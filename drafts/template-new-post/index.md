## The idea

What is this about? A few lines is plenty. Write it badly.

## Why it is worth writing

What made this annoying, surprising, or hard to find an answer for? If the honest
answer is "it isn't", stop here and delete the draft — that is a success, not a
failure.

## The war story

Where did this actually come up in real work? Which project, what broke, what
constraint forced the solution. This is usually the part that makes the post
worth reading rather than a rewrite of the docs.

## Who it is for

What does the reader already know, and what can they do afterwards that they
could not do before?

## Notes and scraps

Links, code that needs to end up in the post, half-sentences, things to check,
arguments with yourself. No structure required at this stage.
